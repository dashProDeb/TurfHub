About This Turf

The Green Arena is Dhaka's premier 5-a-side football ground, built on FIFA-standard artificial turf. Established in 2019, we have hosted over 200 tournaments and welcome 500+ teams annually. The ground features high-output LED floodlights for perfect evening play, a dedicated referee's room, and an on-site sports café. We offer hourly slots and package deals for regular teams.
